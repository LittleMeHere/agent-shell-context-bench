## 1. Bottom line

The V1 defects have been diagnosed and most collection machinery has been
repaired, but the methodology is not yet frozen. The task population,
programmatic outcome, H2/H4 scope, primary-label rule, and collection-order
direction are accepted. The remaining researcher choices are narrower but
still load-bearing: the exact D-005 interval/resampling/fallback, two coder
backends, V2 coder evidence/post-processing contract, staged-audit thresholds
and cap, resource cap/inter-trial delay after macOS, and final amendment/
matrix/plan/tag acceptance.

The recommended design concentrates confirmatory force where the benchmark
can support a real decision:

- estimate the task-weighted Windows-context penalty as an absolute risk
  difference over a qualified, domain-balanced capability roster;
- classify that penalty against a prospectively justified five-percentage-
  point decision threshold;
- retain H3 as the registered within-Windows ordering analysis;
- retain H2 and H4 as fully specified exploratory mechanism analyses, not
  underpowered threshold claims;
- use a finite-roster primary interpretation and separately display domain,
  family, configuration, and time sensitivity; and
- remove agy's unobservable “task-completing command” rule from H1 so every
  harness is judged by the same observable task state.

The researcher accepted this slate on 2026-08-09 before pilot or confirmatory
outcome access. `V2_ACCEPTED_DECISIONS.md` is the canonical acceptance record.
This package remains the evidence summary; it does not silently rewrite the
frozen V1 files, and evidence-dependent parameters remain open until the V2
amendment and tag pass their gates.

## 3. Exact H1 decision language

For the accepted finite capability roster, define:

```text
RD = P(failure | Windows context) - P(failure | Linux context)
delta_RD = 0.05
```

Using the pre-specified 95% interval `[L, U]` from the accepted D-005
implementation:

- **decision-relevant context penalty:** `L > 0.05`;
- **bounded below the decision threshold:** `U < 0.05`;
- **inconclusive:** `L <= 0.05 <= U`.

The paper must also report both marginal failure rates, RR, domain/family
heterogeneity, and leave-one-domain-out sensitivity. “Bounded below five
points” means only that this benchmark does not motivate a broad context-gap
investigation under the stated use. It does not prove no domain-specific
mechanism or generalize beyond the frozen task/model/environment roster.

If every pre-specified integrity gate passes, a narrow below-threshold result
cannot be relabeled “weak methods” after the fact. A wide interval remains
inconclusive. Only a named provenance, measurement, context-fidelity,
completion, cap, or backend-drift gate can make the result uninterpretable.

## 4. Pilot role under the recommendation

The pilot remains scientifically useful, but its role changes from estimating
a named Windows/Linux effect for adaptive N selection to testing whether the
instrument and collection system can support the registered inference.

The blinded pilot should answer:

1. Are there at least five failures and five successes, spread across at least
   two families on each side?
2. Are failures/successes pathologically concentrated by family or domain?
3. Do task predicates, transcript capture, validity attribution, and rubric
   evidence pass their pre-specified integrity gates?
4. Are invalid rates and nuisance heterogeneity inside the prospective
   simulation envelope?
5. Does the observed resource use remain inside the accepted cap?

It must not reveal named context effects or choose tasks, N, coders, or models
because they produce a preferred result. A task-bank change after the pilot
requires a fresh pilot for the changed families.

## 6. Confidence

- **High:** the five-task roster cannot support the intended broad null;
  D-012 must be exact-prompt-set; and agy's unobservable command-purpose rule
  should not alter H1.
- **Moderate to high:** a five-point absolute-gap rule over a qualified
  domain-balanced finite roster matches the intended decision better than the
  V1 1.5x rule.
- **Moderate:** split N is the best breadth/cost direction and the
  five-event/two-family gate is the best tested pilot rule.
- **Moderate:** H2 should be exploratory under current operating
  characteristics; new evidence could justify a later dedicated H2 study.
- **Low to moderate:** the exact affordable N, primary interval, coder sample
  sizes, and human-review burden until the paid shakedown and final D-005/
  D-010 implementations exist.
